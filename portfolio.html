Selected Work

Most of the work that I do is proprietary. Designing learning for organizations means the work usually stays with them. That said, I do have a few items that I'm able to share here that give a sense of how I think about instructional design, technology, and the learner experience.

Rise 360: Accessibility:

Coming Soon! A course focused on accessible design principles and practices for learning professionals. Built in Articulate Rise 360, this piece reflects my belief that accessibility isn't a compliance checkbox, it's a design standard that makes learning better for everyone.

Twine: Technology Integration in the Classroom

A choose-your-own-adventure style activity exploring how educators can make thoughtful decisions about technology integration. Built in Twine, this piece uses branching narrative to put the learner in the decision-making seat rather than just telling them what to do.

A Note on Other Work:

Beyond what's here, I've led design on nationally recognized online programs at Creighton University, managed a portfolio of 80+ learning products at WIDA reaching 200,000 learners annually, and done everything from hand-coding this site to prototyping learning experiences in Unity. If you'd like to talk through specific work in more detail, feel free to reach out.
